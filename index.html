How to Import Stickers to Telegram

Telegram sticker packs are managed via the official @Stickers bot in Telegram. Here is the step-by-step process to upload your styled cat images.

1

Download Your ZIP Pack

Go to the Pack Showcase tab and click Export ZIP Pack. Save the ZIP file locally and extract/unzip the PNG files onto your device.

2

Start the Stickers Bot

Open Telegram and search for @Stickers, or click t.me/Stickers to launch the bot chat. Press Start.

3

Create New Sticker Pack

Send the command /newpack to the bot. It will ask for a name. Type in your desired Pack Title (e.g. My Cute Cats).

4

Upload files & Emojis

The bot will ask you to send the sticker. Upload one of the 512x512px PNG files from your extracted ZIP as a File/Document (NOT a compressed photo). Then send the emoji you mapped to it.

5

Publish the Pack

Repeat Step 4 for all stickers. When finished, send /publish, then /skip (for the icon), and finally type a unique short link URL name (e.g. GJ_cats).

6

Add and Use!

The bot will reply with a sticker link like t.me/addstickers/GJ_cats. Click this link to add your new custom cat stickers to your keyboard!
